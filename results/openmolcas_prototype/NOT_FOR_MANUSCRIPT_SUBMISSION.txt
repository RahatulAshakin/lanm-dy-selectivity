PROTOTYPE-ONLY EVIDENCE — NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR THE LANM CALCULATIONS

This package contains genuine OpenMolcas executions on official test systems.
It does not contain calculations on the manuscript's Dy-LanM or Nd-LanM
structures. It must not be cited as repository reproduction of manuscript
Figures 6–7, project natural occupations, project CASPT2 ordering, project
spin–orbit/anisotropy conclusions, or reduced-cluster sensitivity.

The plots and tables are labeled “prototype only.” Renaming or removing that
qualification would misrepresent their scientific scope.
